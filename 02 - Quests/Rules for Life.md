# 🧭 Rules for Life

One rule is shown on the [[Home|dashboard]] each day (the 🔄 button shuffles). Replace these with your own principles - add or remove list items in the properties.

1. Do the hardest thing first - momentum beats motivation.
2. Never miss twice. One slip is an accident; two is the start of a habit.
3. You become what you repeatedly do - design your environment, not your willpower.
4. Consistency over intensity: 1% better every single day.
5. Sleep, move, eat well. Everything else sits on top of this.
6. Learn in public, write it down, link it - knowledge you don't connect is knowledge you lose.
7. Protect your attention like it is money.
8. Comfort is a slow tax; do one uncomfortable thing every day.
9. Ship imperfect work. Feedback beats polish.
10. Compare yourself only to who you were yesterday.
11. Say no more often so your yes means something.
12. End each day with a shutdown ritual and a plan for tomorrow.
