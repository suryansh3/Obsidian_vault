## Section 4: Question 5

**a) Hardwired Control Unit** The Hardwired Control Unit generates control signals using a fixed logic circuit composed of a step counter, an instruction decoder, and a logic gate matrix that combines IR contents with status flags and timing signals.

**Advantages over Microprogrammed approach:**

- **Higher Speed:** Signals are generated by combinational logic, which is faster than fetching microinstructions from control memory.
    
- **Efficiency:** Can be optimized for a specific, smaller instruction set (common in RISC processors).
